# Third-party data

`src/data/eff-wordlist.json` contains the 7,776-word EFF Long Wordlist,
created by Joseph Bonneau for the Electronic Frontier Foundation (2016).
The numeric dice codes were removed and the words converted to a JSON array;
the word content is unchanged.

Source: https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt
